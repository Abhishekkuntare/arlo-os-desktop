GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

The essentials for choosing, downloading and getting started with ARLO OS.

What is ARLO OS?

ARLO OS is a customizable desktop experience for Windows, designed to bring together desktop personalization, productivity tools, virtual workspaces, developer utilities, and Ghost AI. It offers a more personal way to interact with your Windows PC.

Is ARLO OS a separate operating system?

No. ARLO OS is a desktop application that runs on Windows. It enhances your desktop experience without replacing your existing Windows installation.

Which platforms support ARLO OS?

ARLO OS is currently available for Windows. Check the Microsoft Store listing for the current version and availability. macOS and Linux support should only be described as coming soon if those releases are part of your confirmed roadmap.

Is ARLO OS free to download?

ARLO OS is available through the Microsoft Store. Check the listing for current pricing and installation details.

What features does ARLO OS offer?

ARLO OS is designed around desktop customization, productivity features, virtual workspaces, developer tools, and an optional Ghost AI assistant. Available features may vary by version. Explore the screenshots and interactive preview on this website to learn more.

What is Ghost AI?

Ghost AI is an optional AI assistant designed to help with supported tasks and AI-assisted workflows within ARLO OS. Its capabilities depend on the features available in your version and your AI configuration.

Does Ghost AI require an internet connection?

Gemini-powered chat requires an internet connection and a configured Gemini API key. Optional wake-word detection may run locally on your device. Network use and audio processing depend on the feature you use and its implementation.

Does ARLO OS collect my AI conversations?

Review the ARLO OS privacy policy for details about data collection, storage, and processing. When using online AI services, prompts may be sent to the configured AI provider to generate responses. Avoid sending sensitive information unless you understand the applicable privacy terms.

How do I download and install ARLO OS?

Visit the official Microsoft Store listing to check availability and follow the installation instructions. If a direct installer is offered on this website, follow its accompanying installation instructions.

Where can I get updates for ARLO OS?

Visit the official ARLO OS website and Microsoft Store listing for product information and available updates.
